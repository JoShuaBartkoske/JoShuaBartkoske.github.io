Research Interests

Occultations

Occultations, the covering up of one celestial body by another celestial body, have been used in astronomy for millennia to learn about the sun and moon.

My research focuses on studying asteroid occultations, when an asteroid covers up a star. We observe these occultations with VERITAS and use them to measure the angular diameter of the occulted star.

If you want to learn more about asteroid occultations, here are some good places to start:

  • Good overview of lunar occultation theory (same as asteroid occultations but with the moon): http://spiff.rit.edu/richmond/occult/bessel/bessel.html (Michael Richmond)
  • Good video overview of why we use VERITAS for asteroid occultations: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bMLO1tC6Rtw&t=476s (Michael Daniel)

Gamma-ray binaries

My first project in grad school was working on the gamma-binary LS I +61 303, made up of a Be star and a compact object. The compact object is most probably a pulsar/magnetar but studies still need to be done to determine the nature of the compact object and the mechanism responsible for very high energy gamma rays.
