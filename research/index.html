Research Interests

Gamma-ray binaries

My first project in grad school was working on the gamma-binary LS I +61 303, made up of a Be star and a compact object. The compact object is most probably a pulsar/magnetar but studies still need to be done

Occultations

Occultations, the covering up of one celestial body by another celestial body, have been used in astronomy for millennia to learn about the sun and moon.
